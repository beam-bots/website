Resilience, built into your robot.

An Elixir framework for resilient robot systems. Model robot topology, supervise hardware components, and coordinate sensing and motion.

Define links, joints and motion limits in ordinary Elixir. Beam Bots turns that description into a robot model and a supervision hierarchy.

Keep geometry, units and constraints together. Add sensors and actuators where they belong in the topology.

Hardware, behind a common interface.

03 / Supervise

Give failures a boundary.

Supervision follows the robot’s topology. Components run in separate processes, with OTP supervision to isolate failures and manage restarts.
